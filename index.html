Who am I?

Hello, I'm Niccolò. Currently, I am doing my PhD in Physics in the beautiful city of Marseille.
I am passionate about physics, literature, poetry, politics, a bit of everything in some kinda way.
I have always wanted to create my own webpage. And I know that there will be some more sophisticated
and more beatiful templates, but I think there is a value in making things your own way, even if the
other ways are very much better/easier. I believe in creativity, not in standardisation. If you think about it,
there will always be a more logical way to d things. There is always a way that works for the world, and
there is a way it would work for yourself. I'd rather go for the one that works for myself everytime.
In this regard I think that other sources can be useful, but must not substitute your original thoughts.
This is also my philosophy for using AI for example. Learning without substituting.
If we fill the world with serialized contents, we will lose an important part of who we have always been.
So i'd rather use ChatGPT, for example, as a first basis to have a general flavour about a topic,
but I would never use it to study the topic. I would use it for correcting grammar, but I will always modify
its version. I will use it for coding faster, but... No in that case I may just copy-past if it works (upsi)
Anyway, this is the first thing I will tell you about myself. Hope it is a good first impression :)

    I have always wanted to share as much as possible. here I can share my thoughts, but with an
    important preface. This website is my personal space that I share with who reads. There are
    different spheres in our life: private, work, public. While the private will be private, for many
    people it is hard to make things work between your job and your personal thoughts. I am clearly
    referring to political thinking. I will talk about this in a specific part, but just to show
    quickly my first conclusions: here is the place where I share everything without worrying too much
    that my employer could see this. None of my thoughts should affect my possibility to survive, given
    by my job. Unfortunately, I know that not for everyone is like this. I have the privilege to be able
    to share my thoughts without fearing any drawback. Not everyone does. But I think everyone should have
    the same possibility. This, I think, is one of the key of identifying a privilege. Also: should not make
    a big story out of everything, even tho everything could be a big story.

    What physics told me (to be put on its own link)

    Physics told me two important things about life, that we could enucleate in two words: changes and
    scales or approximation. Everything in life is in a changing state. Almost nothing, or probably
    nothing at all can exists unchanged. Many would say that the first objective in science is to
    describe the natural world at some level of precision. In physics this translates into a very clear
    mathematical object: partial differential equations (PDE). PDE describe the world, because they
    describe how functions change, and functions describe everything. I have always found very fascinating
    the idea that a function is defined as a connection between two elements of sets. This is also linked
    to my fascination for the idea of the collaborative universe, of the fact that there is only interaction,
    there is not such a thing as "passive observer". Even tho, linking this lack of a passive observer to
    souls and inner selfs could be a bit demanding logically, and I don't know if I would agree about it,
    thus I will stick to the idea that every point of the space-time is a passive observer. Maybe I will
    write about this in the future. However,
